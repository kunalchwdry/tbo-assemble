# Guided investment workflow

The overview organizes existing features into four steps: prepare inputs, assess demand, allocate the budget and review risks and supplier information. The lead opportunity is the selected row with the highest model objective (expected scenario profit minus the risk penalty), not the row with the highest forecast or guaranteed return. Zero-allocation rows are not selected; ties are ordered by ID. The panel shows that row's quantity, unit costs and prices, full spend including the configured fixed fee, expected modeled profit, and low modeled profit. Hotel room-nights and flight seats are counted separately.

Source labels identify the initial synthetic dataset or imported inputs, not independently verified provenance. A synthetic dataset may be manually edited. The displayed review-date warning compares the selected review date with the current UTC date. Selected quote dates at or before today are highlighted conservatively. Date-only expiry, the user's clock and all supplier data remain unverified; no warning does not mean a quote is live. The budget warning identifies input edits not applied to the planner. Fitted or attention scenarios may be active but are not validated forecasts. The plan remains subject to its model's limitations and makes no purchase or supplier call. Consult the allocation, demand validation and quote review documentation for details.

## Local decision-review JSON

The overview can download a local JSON review containing the original inventory, the effective model inputs after any currently eligible demand/attention adjustments, budget, risk, date, optimizer result, eligible attention samples and validation summaries. The calculation is refreshed when the user clicks download, using the device clock; an edited budget must first be applied. The review contains all opportunities even if the screen's opportunity list is filtered. It may contain user-entered sensitive data, so handle it accordingly.

This file is not a durable audit log, signed record, approval, supplier offer, reservation or purchase. It does not include the complete historical training data or an independently authenticated review checklist. After an explicit quote application, the current workspace session retains that user-entered quote and transferred values; the downloaded JSON includes the quote identity, written terms, additional cost, application time, and a check of whether the transferred fields still match. It also labels whether the supplied dates have expired using the device clock. These are unverified user inputs and a local self-check, not proof of an offer or approval. Quote handoffs are kept in memory only, cleared on reload, inventory replacement or saved-plan loading, and are not included in saved plans. Clearing handoff details does not undo copied planner values. It does not verify assumptions, measure actual profits or guarantee model quality. The current application model version is recorded; future software versions may behave differently.

## Readiness cues

The overview surfaces actionable input cues for data provenance, experimental demand evidence, budget/review date, session quote handoffs, and risk. Counts refer only to opportunities with nonzero modeled allocation. A cleared cue is not verification or approval: imported inputs and supplier terms remain unverified, fitted demand validation does not validate the allocation, and stress/cancellation cases are uncalibrated. The risk cue deliberately always calls for human review. A session quote handoff reports only whether copied fields still match, not whether the complete plan or checklist has stayed unchanged. Changing budget, dates, demand or risk may change the plan and requires a fresh manual quote impact review even if the copied fields match. The device clock governs the displayed expiry. The local decision-review download includes these cues at the moment of export, but this is not an audit or approval.
