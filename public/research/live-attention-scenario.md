# Experimental live destination attention scenario

This opt-in mode fetches Wikimedia English Wikipedia daily pageviews for seven explicitly supported destination articles (Goa, Dubai, Bali, Jaipur, Kerala, Singapore and Bangkok), then applies a deliberately hypothetical, capped adjustment to the existing per-row `signal` assumption. It is **not** a trained model, nor evidence that pageviews predict bookings, prices, demand or profit. Pageviews are general attention across the world, not travel searches or TBO customer activity. For example, a news story can change pageviews without changing travel demand.

For a matching destination with exactly 14 valid, consecutive daily counts, compare the latest seven total with the preceding seven: `change = recent / previous - 1`. Previous total must be at least 100. The experimental adjustment is `1 + clamp(0.10 * change, -0.10, +0.10)`. Multiply the existing manual signal by this factor, capped to the permitted 0–5 range. The 10% sensitivity and cap are explicit judgmental assumptions, not fitted estimates. Unmatched, stale, malformed, missing, future or low-count data does not adjust the model. Latest observation must be yesterday or at most three days old; response fetch timestamp must be no more than two hours old. Data is used only when the planner's review date equals today's UTC date, to prevent accidental hindsight. Refresh while enabled runs every 30 minutes; errors drop affected samples rather than keeping stale data. This browser-clock-based safeguard is not a trusted audit system.

The scenario feeds the same allocation, coverage, stress and planning API calculations. When disabled, the original inputs return. Saved plans preserve the original manual inputs, not the live data, its timestamp or adjusted multiplier; reopening a plan starts with the scenario off. Exports reflect current scenario calculations and include signal mode and multipliers but are not a reproducible audit record. A plan may change on a later refresh. Holidays, weather, news and fares remain separate context. No supplier inventory is verified, reserved or purchased. Validate with representative timestamped histories, provenance and leakage-free holdouts before relying on any effect for operational decisions.
